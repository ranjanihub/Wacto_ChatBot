Wacto Chatbot - Iframe & Embed Test Page

This page demonstrates how to embed the Wacto Chatbot widget on any website.

Method 1: Direct Iframe Embedding

Renders the exact chatbot widget inside a fixed container or iframe on your site:

<iframe 
  src="https://your-vercel-domain.vercel.app/widget" 
  width="380" 
  height="630" 
  style="border: none; border-radius: 16px; box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.2);"
  title="Wacto AI Chatbot">
</iframe>

Method 2: One-Line Script Embedding

Injects the floating launcher icon in the bottom-right corner of any site and opens the chatbot frame when clicked:

<script src="https://your-vercel-domain.vercel.app/widget.js" defer></script>
